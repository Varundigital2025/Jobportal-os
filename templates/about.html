About Our Platform

Connecting Exceptional Talent with Leading Opportunities

JobPortal is engineered as a modern, local-first web platform for career discovery, company hiring, and streamlined talent workflows.

Our Mission & Architecture

Built from the ground up on Python 3 and Flask without external server dependencies like XAMPP or Apache. JobPortal provides a fast, secure, and production-grade architecture supporting candidate applications, employer job management, and administrative platform governance.

Robust SQLite relational database with SQLAlchemy ORM
Role-based access security for Candidates, Employers & Admins
Secure file uploads and resume verification system

Platform Pillars

For Job Seekers

Discover verified roles, track application statuses in real time, and showcase your portfolio.

For Employers

Manage jobs effortlessly, review candidate profiles, and organize the recruitment funnel.

For Administrators

Full platform metrics, user moderation, job compliance, and operational control.
